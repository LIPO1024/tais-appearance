# Free and open-source project policy

TAIS Appearance is a free and open-source project maintained for the Zotero community. The maintainers do not charge users and do not operate a paid edition.

## No payment collection by the maintainers

- All bundled themes and all core features are available to everyone at no cost.
- There is no paid edition, subscription, membership, activation key, theme lock, or feature lock.
- The project does not collect donations, sponsorship payments, license fees, or other payments.
- The plugin and product website do not include payment SDKs, checkout flows, payment credentials, or commercial activation services.
- Users receive the same functionality regardless of identity, location, or contribution history.

## License rights remain governed by the license

The project includes code derived from Mica for Zotero and is distributed under the applicable GNU AGPL terms. Upstream copyright notices, license terms, and corresponding-source obligations are preserved.

This policy describes how the TAIS maintainers distribute and sustain this project. It does **not** add a non-commercial restriction to the AGPL or narrow rights granted by the license. Downstream users retain the rights and obligations stated in the applicable license.

## Project sustainability

Maintenance is supported through non-financial community contributions: bug reports, reproducible test cases, documentation improvements, translation review, compatibility testing, and code contributions through GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.

No payment is required or requested to participate.

## Scope

This policy applies to the TAIS Appearance plugin, its bundled themes, documentation, and official product showcase. Any future change to the maintainers' distribution policy will be documented publicly and will not retroactively restrict rights already granted under applicable licenses.

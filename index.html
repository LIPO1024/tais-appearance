APPEARANCE
DESIGNED AS A SYSTEM

Everything belongs to one visual language.

Glass is the material, readability is the rule. Interface text and PDF content stay separate.

01

12 curated Glass presets

Six light and six dark presets with distinct tone, surface hierarchy, borders, and accents.

02

Typography that respects reading

Independent controls for Zotero interface text and annotation cards. PDF-embedded fonts are untouched.

03

Protected PDF experience

The plugin styles the surrounding environment, not PDF glyphs, canvas pixels, or native selection.

04

Reversible previews

Preview, compare, apply, cancel, undo, and restore without rewriting library content.

05

Local backgrounds

Built-in Stage Mist and custom images are processed locally. No image upload is required.

06

Profiles and Safe Mode

Back up settings, import scoped profiles, inspect conflicts, and return to native Zotero.

THE GLASS COLLECTION

Twelve moods. One consistent system.

Choose a theme to preview its visual character. The sample swatches are illustrative; the installed extension computes final surfaces from the selected theme and contrast safeguards.

READING SURFACES

Transparent surroundings. Stable text.

The page surround and page surface are controlled separately. This demo shows the principle, not a live PDF rendering.

Paper surface
The page surface remains stable enough for long-form reading. Text is not blurred, shadowed, duplicated, or recolored by the Glass layer.
